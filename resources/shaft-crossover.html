How the match works

ProfileDriver shafts are sorted from EMB's 1 to 5 bend ratings. Butt-stiff means the handle rates at least one point firmer than the tip, tip-stiff the reverse, and mid-stiff means the mid section is the firmest of the three. Only shafts in the chosen profile are listed.
WeightThe rated driver shafts are split into thirds by weight, so lighter, mid and heavier always mean the same slice of the database. Shafts outside the band drop down the list by how far off they are.
FlexClubhead speed sets which driver flexes are in play, using the same gates as the Shaft Map. Inside that window, the flex that matches the iron shaft ranks first.

EMB Golf Academy · Bend ratings are EMB's own 1 to 5 scale for comparing shafts across brands.
