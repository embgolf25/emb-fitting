How to read this

Face to backCG depth, rated 1 (forward) to 5 (back). The numbers on the sole mark each rating. Forward heads spin less and launch lower. Deep heads launch higher and are more stable on mishits.
Heel to toeShot-shape bias. CG toward the heel helps the face close, which favors a draw. Toward the toe favors a fade.
Height above the soleLow, mid or high, taken from each head's CG label. Lower CG generally means less spin.
Grouped dotsHeads with the same ratings sit in a small ring. The spacing inside a ring is only there so you can see each one.

Ratings come from the EMB Fitting equipment database. Head shape shown is a generic 460cc right-handed profile, not any one model.
