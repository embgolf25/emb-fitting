How the lists are built

ProfileDriver shafts are sorted from EMB's 1 to 5 bend ratings: butt-stiff when the handle rates at least a point firmer than the tip, tip-stiff the reverse, mid-stiff when the mid section is the firmest of the three. Iron shafts use the profile from the EMB tour Irons sheet.
WeightDriver shafts are split into thirds by weight across the rated database. Iron bands are light under 120g, normal 120 to 125g, heavy over 125g. Iron shafts without a weight on the sheet stay listed at the bottom, marked.
FlexEither slider sets the flex window for both lists, using the Shaft Map's driver gates. The 7-iron slider follows driver speed at 80%, a general guide until the sheet carries a 7-iron rule. Project X numbers read as 6.0 S, 6.5 X, 7.0 TX, and S+ counts as S or X.
